Brazil - published guidance for officials who decide visa, residence, naturalisation
and refugee cases
Checked 2026-11-15. Script: ../download_br.py (re-run: python3 download_br.py)
Current state: 254 files, 160 MB, acts dated 1987-2026; 15 guidance, 239 procedural acts.

Plain statement
---------------
Brazil publishes very little true case-officer guidance. Almost everything an officer
works from is a numbered legal act: resoluções normativas of the two collegiate bodies
(CONARE for refugees, CNIg for labour migration), ministerial and interministerial
portarias, and Federal Police portarias. These are published in the official gazette
and are detailed enough to serve as the procedure manual, which is why 239 of the 254
files here are flagged "procedural act".

Real guidance that is published (15 files, kind = guidance):
- 9 CONARE technical notes (notas técnicas, 2019-2021): country-of-origin studies on
  Venezuela (with addendum and two extensions), Syria, Iraq, Afghanistan, Mali and
  Burkina Faso. Each tells the committee and its case officers whether "serious and
  generalised violation of human rights" (art. 1 III of Law 9.474/1997) applies to a
  nationality, and so whether claims are decided by the simplified group procedure.
  This is the closest thing Brazil has to country policy notes.
- 1 SISCONARE manual for the Federal Police (version 8, February 2021): step-by-step
  instructions for police staff who receive and register asylum claims.
- 1 Manual do Serviço Consular e Jurídico, 2010 edition (326 pages, chapter 12 on
  visas): the Foreign Ministry's binding instructions to consulates. The ministry does
  not publish it; the copy comes from FGV, which organised the ministry's entrance exam
  and posted the manual as study material. It is 16 years old and partly superseded by
  the 2017 Migration Law.
- 4 handbooks from the Justice Ministry's digital library: two 2016 guides for
  front-line federal staff in border areas (MT Brasil project, with ICMPD), a 2007
  casebook of annotated CONARE decisions, and a 2009 write-up of the reception post for
  deported and non-admitted persons at Guarulhos airport. Older and softer than the
  rest, kept because they are addressed to staff, not applicants.

What exists but is not published
- Federal Police internal instructions: Instruções Normativas of the Director-General
  (e.g. IN 142/2018 on migration registration, IN 198/2021 on infraction proceedings)
  and the CGPI's Mensagens Oficiais-Circulares to immigration police units. They are
  cited in decisions posted on gov.br/pf, but the texts are in the internal service
  bulletin. Three circulars on seafarers circulate on a shipping insurer's website
  (listed in not-obtained.tsv, not downloaded: not an official source).
- The current consular regulations and the Itamaraty's circular telegrams on visas.
- No manual of the Departamento de Migrações (DEMIG) for residence or naturalisation
  case officers was found; DEMIG publishes checklists and FAQs for applicants only.

Sources used
- https://bibliotecadigital.mj.gov.br (also reached as dspace.mj.gov.br): the Justice
  Ministry's digital library. Holds gazette copies of the ministry's portarias and of
  CNIg and CONARE resolutions, with catalogue records. Its search is closed to robots,
  so the script walks 83 subject headings (Refugiado, Estrangeiro, Visto temporário,
  Autorização de residência, Naturalização, Acolhida humanitária, ...): 281 records,
  of which 196 acts and 4 handbooks were kept. Left out: laws and decrees, studies,
  books, applicant guides, one individual expulsion order, fee tables.
  -> biblioteca-mj/atos/ and biblioteca-mj/guias/
- https://www.gov.br/mj/pt-br/assuntos/seus-direitos/refugio (CONARE pages):
  technical notes -> conare-notas-tecnicas/; Federal Police manual -> sisconare/;
  CONARE resolutions 1-27 and 31 as posted, several in consolidated form, including
  RN 18 consolidated up to RN 33 (.docx), the main asylum-procedure text
  -> conare-resolucoes/; portarias and despachos -> mj-portarias/.
- https://www.gov.br/pf/pt-br/assuntos/imigracao/lei-de-migracao/publicacoes/cgpi:
  two Federal Police portarias on migration time limits -> pf/. The other folders of
  that section hold notices and decisions in individual cases (names, fines) and were
  not downloaded.
- https://conhecimento.fgv.br/sites/default/files/concursos/mre/MSCJ_completo-1.pdf
  -> mre/
- Also read, nothing for this collection: gov.br/mj "migracoes" pages (naturalisation,
  statelessness, labour-immigration portal: applicant information, forms, links to the
  gazette), CONARE "publicações" (leaflets, statistics).

Overlap: some CONARE resolutions are present twice, once from the CONARE page
(often the consolidated or annotated text) and once from the library (the gazette
text). Both were kept; the index shows the source of each.

Not obtained (see not-obtained.tsv)
- Acts that the CONARE pages link only to the official gazette (www.in.gov.br):
  the gazette answered HTTP 403 to this machine (robots.txt included), so it was not
  used. This affects CONARE resolutions 28, 29, 30, 32 and 33 as separate files (32
  and 33 came from the library; all are reflected in the consolidated RN 18) and a
  number of interministerial portarias, most of which came from the library instead.
- portaldeimigracao.mj.gov.br (CNIg / OBMigra portal): reset the connection three
  times in a row, robots.txt unreadable; not used. The CNIg resolutions came from the
  library, whose holdings may lag behind the portal.
- www.gov.br/mre: robots.txt disallows every PDF under /mre/, so the visa-regime table
  (Quadro Geral de Regime de Vistos) and other consular PDFs were not fetched.
- www.planalto.gov.br answered HTTP 429 on robots.txt; not needed (laws and decrees
  are out of scope).
- CONARE plenary minutes (about 150 PDFs): skipped on purpose, not instructions.
- web.archive.org was not contacted for Brazil.

Found but not yet downloaded (second pass, 2026-11-15)
- The Portal de Imigração (portaldeimigracao.mj.gov.br, CNIg and the labour-immigration
  unit CGIg) cannot be reached from this machine, but the Internet Archive holds about
  3,450 of its PDFs (CDX listing saved to listings/ on the next run). Among them:
  * the CNIg resoluções normativas under the 2017 Migration Law, nos. 2-51 (most of which
    the Justice Ministry library lacks), joint CNIg/CONARE resolutions, DEMIG portarias
    (GAB-DEMIG 4/2020, 2/2021, 3/2021) and a few Federal Police portarias;
  * real staff guidance of the labour-immigration unit: eight Ordens de Serviço (CGIg,
    2004-2018) and technical/information notes (e.g. Nota Técnica 003/2018 on criminal-
    record certificates, Nota Técnica 3/2025, Notas Informativas 001/2018 and 02/2020).
  The script has a new source "archive" (web.archive.org, 4 s spacing) that takes these,
  skipping acts already obtained from the library, plus archived gazette pages for CONARE
  RN 30/2019, PF Portaria 8.728/2018 and PF Portaria 28/2022-DIREX. In this pass the run
  of the script was refused by the operator's permission system, so none of this was
  fetched; the items are listed in not-obtained.tsv. Run: python3 download_br.py archive
  (then a full run to rebuild index.tsv).
- Despachos of CONARE linked to the gazette are notices to named applicants in individual
  cases and are left out on purpose; two Education Council resolutions and an Operação
  Acolhida committee resolution linked from the CONARE pages are outside scope.

Format notes
- index.tsv columns: file, title, date, source_url, kind, title_en, issuing_body.
- Two library records (handles 884 and 905) carry the same file name PRT_GM_1994_702.pdf
  (Portaria MJ 702/1994: the forms, and the procedure for naturalisation). The file on
  disk is record 884; the script now saves 905 as ..._h905.pdf on the next full run.
- No document seen carries a confidentiality notice. The 2010 consular manual is an
  official text republished by FGV for an exam; it has no copyright notice of its own.

Completeness
- The library is catalogued by hand; an act with unusual subject headings would be
  missed by the subject walk. Coverage of CNIg resolutions after 2021 looks thin
  (Resolutions 42, 43, 45, 47 and 50 are there; 46, 49 and 51, which exist, are not).
- The library server dropped connections during the first run after about 330
  requests at 1.2 s spacing; the script now waits 3 s between requests to it and the
  second run completed without errors.
- Dates are the date of the act (catalogue field "created"), not of publication.
