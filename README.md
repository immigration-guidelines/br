# Immigration guidance: Brazil

Published guidance that the authorities of Brazil issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 258 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

Brazil publishes very little true case-officer guidance: almost everything an officer works from is a numbered legal act published in the official gazette (resolutions of the refugee committee CONARE and the labour-migration council CNIg, ministerial and Federal Police orders), so 239 of the 254 files are marked `procedural act`. The 15 guidance files are CONARE's technical notes on whether serious and generalised human-rights violations apply to Venezuela, Syria, Iraq, Afghanistan, Mali and Burkina Faso (2019 to 2021), the Federal Police manual for registering asylum claims (2021), the Foreign Ministry's consular and legal service manual (2010, chapter 12 on visas) and four handbooks for front-line staff.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
